

一、会议开场 & 流程说明

1. Good morning/afternoon everyone. Thank you for joining today’s Post Incident Review meeting.

2. Today we will conduct a thorough review of the production incident that occurred on [date/time].

3. The objectives of this meeting are: to clarify what happened, identify root causes, analyze impacts, and finalize corrective and preventive actions.

4. Let’s keep the discussion focused, fact-based, and blame-free. This is a learning session, not a fault-finding one.

5. I’ll guide us through the agenda: incident recap → timeline verification → impact assessment → root cause analysis → action items → follow-up plan.

二、事故描述 & 时间线还原

1. Let’s start with a quick recap of the incident.

2. The incident was detected at [time] when [symptom, e.g., system downtime, service outage, performance drop].

3. The issue affected [affected systems/services/users].

4. Let’s walk through the timeline step by step to ensure accuracy.

5. At [time], the first alert was triggered.

6. The on-call team responded at [time] and began troubleshooting.

7. The service was partially restored at [time] and fully recovered at [time].

8. To confirm: is there any missing or incorrect information in the timeline?

9. What were the key observations during the incident?

三、影响 & 损失评估

1. Let’s summarize the business and technical impacts of the incident.

2. The incident caused [duration] of service unavailability.

3. It impacted [number] of users, customers, or business processes.

4. There was no data loss / Partial data loss / Temporary data inconsistency occurred.

5. The estimated business impact includes [revenue loss, customer complaints, SLA violation].

6. Did the incident lead to any secondary failures or cascading issues?

四、根因分析 (RCA)

1. Now let’s move into root cause analysis. We will use the 5-Whys method.

2. What directly caused the incident?

3. Why did this problem happen?

4. Why didn’t the monitoring/alerting system catch it earlier?

5. Why didn’t the preventive mechanism work?

6. The immediate cause was [direct cause].

7. The root cause is identified as [root cause, e.g., human error, configuration error, code bug, infrastructure failure, insufficient monitoring].

8. We need to distinguish between contributing factors and root cause.

9. Are there any other hidden causes we haven’t covered?

五、应急处置复盘

1. Let’s review how the team responded during the incident.

2. The response actions taken included [troubleshooting steps, rollback, restart, failover].

3. What was done well in the response?

4. What could have been handled more efficiently?

5. Was the communication during the incident clear and timely?

6. Did we follow the incident response procedure correctly?

六、整改 & 预防措施 (Action Items)

1. Based on the root cause, we propose the following corrective and preventive actions.

2. We need to define clear owner, deadline, and priority for each action.

3. Action 1: [Short-term fix] – Owner: [Name] – Deadline: [Date]

4. Action 2: [Long-term prevention] – Owner: [Name] – Deadline: [Date]

5. Action 3: [Improve monitoring/alerting] – Owner: [Name] – Deadline: [Date]

6. Action 4: [Update runbook/process/training] – Owner: [Name] – Deadline: [Date]

7. All actions must be trackable and verifiable.

8. We will schedule a follow-up meeting to check the progress of all actions.

七、会议提问 & 确认

1. Does anyone have additional findings or insights to share?

2. Are there any different perspectives on the root cause?

3. Let’s confirm the final conclusions and action items.

4. Is everyone clear about their responsibilities and deadlines?

5. If no further questions, let’s finalize the meeting notes and distribute them within 24 hours.

八、会议结束

1. Thank you all for your valuable input today.

2. Let’s ensure all actions are implemented on time to avoid similar incidents in the future.

3. The meeting is adjourned.
